**Dr. Nasy’an Taufiq Al Ghifari** is a distinguished academician and researcher specializing in **Data Mining**, **Graph Mining**, **Social Computing**, and **Big Data Analytics** in Indonesia. He obtained his B.Eng. (Bachelor of Engineering) from Telkom University, followed by M.Eng. (Master of Engineering) and Dr. (Doctor of Computer Science) from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), Indonesia. His career is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD), developing advanced algorithms for processing complex (un)structured data, and leveraging human-computer interaction (UX/HCI) and data visualization to solve critical challenges in modern social and digital ecosystems.  

Dr. Nasy’an Taufiq Al Ghifari serves as an assistant professor at the Department of Information Technology, Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta (UMY), Indonesia. He leads the Data Science & AI Research Laboratory at UMY, directing cutting-edge research at the intersection of network science and social computing. As a prominent expert in Graph Mining and Big Data Analytics, he has devoted years of academic endeavor to pioneering novel dynamic graph neural networks (GNNs), contextual bipartite embeddings, and graph-based community detection algorithms (such as HCD-LEAM and DHCD-iLEAM) aimed at decoding the hidden structures, dynamic behaviors, and evolutionary patterns within massive, interconnected complex networks.  

Dr. Nasy’an Taufiq Al Ghifari is actively engaged in academic dissemination, research mentorship, and industrial-academic collaborations. He has authored and co-authored numerous peer-reviewed articles featured in reputable international journals and conferences indexed by Scopus, Web of Science, and SINTA. His research contributions appear in leading outlets such as Social Network Analysis and Mining (CiteScore: 6.4), Advances in Engineering Software (CiteScore: 12.6), Journal of ICT Research & Applications (CiteScore: 2.8), and International Journal of Electrical & Computer Engineering (CiteScore: 4.1). His ongoing research agenda focuses on applying Data Mining, Graph Mining, and Social Computing across diverse real-world domains, including:  

* Dynamic Heterogeneous Graph Mining: Developing adaptive local exploration algorithms (HCD-LEAM, DHCD-iLEAM) for community detection and structural trajectory analysis in rapidly evolving networks.
* Social Computing & NLP: Building human-validated benchmarks, explainable fake news detection models, and multi-task aspect-based sentiment analysis systems for large-scale Indonesian social and e-commerce text.
* Big Data Analytics for Environmental & Societal Systems: Mining multitemporal spatial trajectories to detect deforestation, modeling global aircraft accident networks, and uncovering latent energy consumption profiles via graph clustering.
* Predictive Machine Learning & Intelligent UX: Harnessing XGBoost, Random Forest, and GNNs for time-series forecasting, talent mapping, and analyzing user behavior patterns in digital systems.
